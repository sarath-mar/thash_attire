# Fashion Storefront Design

## Pages
- Homepage
- Shop / All Products
- Category Collections
- Product Details
- Shopping Cart
- Checkout
- Order Tracking
- About Thash Attire
- Contact

## Homepage
Create an editorial fashion experience:
- Large hero section with campaign photography.
- New arrivals.
- Featured collections.
- Best sellers.
- Lifestyle photography.
- Brand story.
- Instagram-inspired gallery.
- Newsletter and footer.

## Product Listing
- Large product photography.
- Clean product cards.
- Product name and price.
- Available sizes.
- Color variations.
- Filters and sorting.
- Quick product discovery.

## Product Details
- Large image gallery.
- Product name and pricing.
- Fabric and material information.
- Size selection.
- Quantity selection.
- Add to cart.
- Delivery information.
- Related products.

## Conversion UX
- Clear shopping actions.
- Visible cart status.
- Simple checkout.
- Clear order confirmation.
- Mobile-friendly interactions.

Never sacrifice usability for visual effects.
